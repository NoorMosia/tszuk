# Turning a Brand Client into Recurring Revenue

**The goal:** Move from a once-off project delivery to an ongoing partnership where you're embedded in their business as their creative and technical arm.

---

## Strategy 1: Brand Maintenance Retainer (Easiest Sell)

The brand you built needs to stay alive. Offer a monthly retainer that covers:

- Minor website updates (new case studies, copy tweaks, image swaps)
- Seasonal homepage refreshes
- Dependency and hosting maintenance
- Priority response time (vs. quoting from scratch each time)

**Pricing:**
| Market | Monthly |
|--------|---------|
| South Africa | R1,600 – R3,800/mo |
| Western | $300 – $800/mo |

**Why they buy it:** They don't want to hunt for a dev every time they need a small change, and they know you already understand their system inside out.

---

## Strategy 2: Content & Social Media Direction

You built their tone of voice and visual language. You're the best person to maintain consistency across channels.

- Monthly social media templates (Stories, posts, carousels)
- Content calendar direction
- Caption writing in their brand voice
- Campaign creative for product drops or events
- Template updates for seasonal campaigns

**Pricing:**
| Market | Monthly |
|--------|---------|
| South Africa | R5,000 – R12,000/mo |
| Western | $1,000 – $3,000/mo |

**Why they buy it:** In-house teams struggle to maintain the quality you established. You become their creative arm rather than a vendor they once hired.

---

## Strategy 3: Website Analytics + Quarterly Optimisation Sprints

Set up privacy-friendly analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics, Plausible, or Umami), then offer quarterly reports with actionable improvements:

- Performance audits and speed improvements
- Conversion rate optimisation on the contact form
- SEO adjustments based on real traffic data
- New sections or features based on what's performing
- Competitor monitoring for positioning adjustments

**Pricing:**
| Market | Quarterly |
|--------|-----------|
| South Africa | R4,500 – R8,000/quarter |
| Western | $1,000 – $2,000/quarter |

**Why they buy it:** They see results tied to data, not guesswork. You shift from a creative expense to a growth investment.

---

## Strategy 4: Additional Pages & Features (Roadmap Upsell)

After launch, plant seeds for what comes next. The single-page site is a starting point — not the destination:

- Individual case study pages with detailed project breakdowns
- A blog or journal (positions them as thought leaders in their industry)
- E-commerce or booking integration
- A detailed "Work with us" page with a multi-step intake form
- Multi-language support for international clients
- Event landing pages or campaign microsites

**Pricing:** Per project, but framed as a quarterly or biannual roadmap you revisit together.

**Why they buy it:** The brand grows, the site should grow with it. You're already embedded — the switching cost of finding someone new who understands their brand is too high.

---

## Strategy 5: Brand Guardianship (Premium Positioning)

Position yourself as the ongoing custodian of their brand. Not a freelancer they call when something breaks — a partner who proactively ensures consistency:

- Audit any third-party work (print shops, signage vendors, other designers) against brand guidelines
- Approve or produce assets for new touchpoints (packaging, merch, event materials)
- Annual brand health check — is the positioning still relevant? Does the visual language need refreshing?
- Be the first call when they're launching something new
- Quarterly brand consistency reports

**Pricing:**
| Market | Monthly |
|--------|---------|
| South Africa | R6,000 – R15,000/mo |
| Western | $1,500 – $4,000/mo |

**Why they buy it:** High-end clients understand brand consistency = trust = revenue. They pay for peace of mind that nothing goes out the door looking off-brand.

---

## Strategy 6: Referral & Network Revenue

Once you've delivered great work, leverage the relationship:

- Ask for referrals — offer a referral fee (10–15%) or reciprocal introduction
- If the client has a network (other businesses, artists, brands in their space), position yourself as "their brand team" and let word-of-mouth compound
- Offer to white-label services under their umbrella if they operate as a connector in their industry
- Ask for a testimonial and case study permission — these close future clients

---

## How to Package It: Tiered Retainer Model

Don't sell the retainer as a separate awkward conversation months after launch. Build it into the original quotation as a natural Phase 5:

> "Phase 4 delivers the brand into your hands. Phase 5 keeps it growing."

### Post-Launch Partnership Tiers

| Tier | What's Included | Monthly (ZAR) | Monthly (USD) |
|------|-----------------|---------------|---------------|
| **Maintain** | Bug fixes, hosting oversight, 2 small updates/mo, priority support | R2,500 | $500 |
| **Grow** | Everything in Maintain + social templates (4/mo), quarterly analytics report, SEO tweaks | R7,500 | $1,500 |
| **Partner** | Everything in Grow + brand guardianship, proactive strategy sessions, unlimited small requests, first access for new projects | R14,000 | $3,500 |

### Annual Revenue Potential (Per Client)

| Tier | Annual (ZAR) | Annual (USD) |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| Maintain | R30,000 | $6,000 |
| Grow | R90,000 | $18,000 |
| Partner | R168,000 | $42,000 |

With 3–5 clients on retainer, you're looking at a solid recurring base before any new project work comes in.

---

## When to Pitch It

| Moment | What to Say |
|--------|-------------|
| In the original quotation | Include the tiered table as an optional "Post-Launch Partnership" section |
| During the project | Casually mention: "After launch, most clients find it useful to have us on a light retainer for updates — I'll include the options when we wrap up." |
| At handover | "Here's everything you need to run this independently. If you'd rather not think about the technical side, here are three ways we can keep supporting you." |
| 30 days post-launch | "Your free support window closes this week. Here's what ongoing support looks like if you'd like to continue." |

---

## The Pitch Framing

Don't sell hours. Sell outcomes and peace of mind:

- **Bad:** "I can do 4 hours of work per month for R3,800."
- **Good:** "Your brand stays consistent, your site stays fast, and you never have to think about the technical side. R2,500/mo."

Don't sell maintenance. Sell growth:

- **Bad:** "I'll fix bugs and update plugins."
- **Good:** "Every quarter I'll show you what's working, what's not, and what we should build next to grow your reach."

---

## The Core Principle

The project you quoted is a **one-time event**. Recurring revenue comes from framing yourself as the **ongoing relationship** — the person who keeps the brand consistent, the site performing, and the creative evolving.

The strongest position: **make it harder for them to leave you than to stay.**

---

## Quick Wins to Start Immediately

1. Add the tiered retainer table to your `quotation-branding.md` as a Phase 5
2. Set up basic analytics on every site you launch (gives you data to reference in the quarterly pitch)
3. Send a "brand health check" email 90 days after launch — proactive outreach shows you care
4. Create 2–3 social media templates as a bonus at handover — once they see the quality, they'll want more monthly
5. Build a simple internal tracker: which clients are on retainer, when their quarterly check-in is due, what's next on their roadmap

---

*The goal isn't to extract money from the client. It's to become so valuable to their brand's growth that the retainer feels like an obvious decision.*
